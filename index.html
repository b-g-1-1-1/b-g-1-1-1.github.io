TV Launcher

TV Launcher is a home screen app for Android TV and Google TV devices. It shows your apps, a clock and weather, a TV guide, and a row of recent videos from YouTube channels.

YouTube sign-in

You can optionally sign in with a Google account once so the app can fill its YouTube row from the channels you subscribe to. The app asks for read-only access to your YouTube account, reads your list of subscriptions, stores that list on your device, and then gives up its access. You can hide the YouTube row or remove the stored list at any time from the app's menu.
