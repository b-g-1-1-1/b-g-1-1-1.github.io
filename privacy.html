TV Launcher privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What the app accesses

If you choose to sign in with Google, TV Launcher requests read-only access to your YouTube account (the youtube.readonly scope). It uses that access once, to read the list of channels you subscribe to: each channel's ID and name.

How that data is used and stored

The channel list is saved only on your device and is used only to show recent videos from those channels on the home screen. As soon as the list has been read, the app revokes its access and does not keep any sign-in token.

What is not collected

The app has no server. The developer does not receive, store or share any of your data. The app does not read your name, email address, watch history, playlists or any other account data, and it contains no analytics or advertising.

Removing your data

Choose "Remove channel list" in the app's menu, or uninstall the app, to delete the stored list. You can also review and remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

TV Launcher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
